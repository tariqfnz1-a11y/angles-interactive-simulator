# 🎨 Angles & Types — Colourful Study Note

Welcome! This quick, colourful note helps you learn angle names and how to spot them. It's written for 4th graders and teachers — short, fun, and full of examples.

---

## 🔺 What is an angle?
An angle is formed when two lines (called "arms") meet at a point (the "vertex"). We measure angles in degrees (°).

- Vertex: the meeting point
- Arms: the two lines forming the angle
- Unit: degrees (°)

---

## 🌈 Angle types (with colours)

- <span style="color:#E53935">**Acute angle**</span> — less than 90° (small & sharp)
  - Example: 30°, 45°

- <span style="color:#1E88E5">**Right angle**</span> — exactly 90° (perfect corner)
  - Symbol: ⟂

- <span style="color:#FB8C00">**Obtuse angle**</span> — greater than 90° but less than 180° (wide)
  - Example: 120°

- <span style="color:#6A1B9A">**Straight angle**</span> — exactly 180° (a straight line)

- <span style="color:#00897B">**Reflex angle**</span> — greater than 180° but less than 360° (bends the other way)

- <span style="color:#FDD835">**Full/Complete angle**</span> — exactly 360° (all the way round)

---

## ✏️ Quick visual guide

Use this simple text art to imagine angles:

- Acute:  <span style="color:#E53935">∠</span> (looks sharp)
- Right:  <span style="color:#1E88E5">∟</span> (corner)
- Obtuse: <span style="color:#FB8C00">⌒</span> (wide)
- Straight: <span style="color:#6A1B9A">—</span>

---

## 🧠 Tips to recognize angles

- If the angle is smaller than a corner of a book, it is probably acute.
- If it looks like the corner of a square, it is a right angle.
- If it opens more than a right angle but is not a straight line, it is obtuse.

---

## ✅ Practice questions

1. Is 45° acute, right, obtuse, straight, or reflex?
2. Name the angle type for 90°.
3. What type is 200°?
4. What is a straight angle in degrees?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. 45° is an **acute** angle.
2. 90° is a **right** angle.
3. 200° is a **reflex** angle.
4. A straight angle is **180°**.

</details>
